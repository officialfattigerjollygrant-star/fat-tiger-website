Delivery, offers & payments

Update delivery radius, delivery charges, first-order offers, online-payment offers and visible UPI/bank instructions. Razorpay secret keys are configured in Vercel Environment Variables only—never store secret keys here.

Offer rule: first order gets 10% off + free delivery; returning customer gets 10% off for online payment; offers do not stack. Cart ₹399+ gets free delivery. Customer delivery locations outside the maximum radius are blocked. Check outlet coordinates carefully while physically at the restaurant.
Payment setup: This panel saves customer-facing UPI/bank instructions and the online-payment switch. Live Razorpay payments only work after the merchant account is activated and RAZORPAY_KEY_ID + RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET are set in Vercel. The connected settlement bank account is managed in the Razorpay merchant dashboard, not from this website.
